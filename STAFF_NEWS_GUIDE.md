# THA News Posting Guide

## Quick Start: Post News in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Prepare Your Content
- Write a compelling headline (under 60 characters)
- Prepare a 2-3 line excerpt/summary
- Write the full article in rich text (Word, Google Docs, etc.)
- Take or prepare an image (min 1200x630px, max 5MB)

### Step 2: Upload to Airtable
1. Visit: **tha.or.tz/post-news**
2. Fill out the form:
   - **Title**: Your article headline
   - **Excerpt**: 2-3 line summary
   - **Content**: Paste your article (HTML or plain text)
   - **Category**: Select one (Announcements, Events, Press Releases, Success Stories)
   - **Publish Date**: When should this go live?
3. Upload your image using the Cloudinary widget
4. Click **Submit**

### Step 3: Website Updates
- Your article appears on the News page after submission
- Featured articles appear on the homepage
- Newsletter subscribers receive updates automatically
- **Note**: Website rebuilds happen every 15 minutes after submission

## Content Guidelines

### Headlines
- **Length**: 50-60 characters (fits on mobile)
- **Tone**: Action-oriented, clear, compelling
- **Examples**:
  - ✅ "Hepatitis Awareness Campaign Reaches 5,000 in Dar"
  - ✅ "New Mental Health Training Program Launches May 2024"
  - ❌ "Stuff about health" (too vague)
  - ❌ "Tanzania Health Alliance Announces a New Initiative That Will Help People Who Have Diseases" (too long)

### Excerpts (Summary)
- **Length**: 2-3 sentences, under 120 characters
- **Purpose**: Hook readers on the homepage
- **Tone**: Match the headline; be specific
- **Examples**:
  - ✅ "Our comprehensive training prepares community health workers to detect hepatitis early. Applications open April 15."
  - ✅ "Join us for a free mental health awareness workshop in Dar es Salaam next month. Spaces are limited."
  - ❌ "This is about a program" (too vague)

### Full Article Content
- **Length**: 300-1000 words
- **Structure**:
  1. Opening hook or context (why this matters)
  2. Main body (details, quotes, impact)
  3. Call-to-action (what readers should do next)
  4. Contact info or link

### Images
- **Size**: 1200px × 630px (or any 16:9 ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG (will auto-convert to WebP)
- **File size**: Under 5MB
- **Quality**: High contrast, clear subjects
- **Avoid**: Blurry, overly dark, generic stock photos
- **Best**: Real people, real events, authentic moments

### Categories
Select ONE category that best fits:
- **Announcements**: Important news, partnerships, updates
- **Events**: Upcoming workshops, training, conferences
- **Press Releases**: Official statements, media coverage
- **Success Stories**: Impact stories, testimonials, case studies

## Publishing Calendar

### Frequency
- **Minimum**: 1 article per month
- **Recommended**: 2-3 articles per month
- **Peak times**: Mondays and Wednesdays for maximum engagement

### Seasonal Focus
- **Jan-Mar**: New year initiatives, World Health Day (April)
- **Apr-Jun**: Summer health programs, HIV prevention month (June)
- **Jul-Sep**: Back-to-school health education, World Hepatitis Day (July)
- **Oct-Dec**: Year-end impact, holiday campaigns

## Troubleshooting

### Q: My article didn't appear on the website
**A**: 
- Check your email for confirmation
- Website rebuilds every 15 minutes; try refreshing after 5 min
- Contact the tech team if still missing

### Q: Can I edit an article after publishing?
**A**: 
- Go back to the form at tha.or.tz/post-news
- Find your article and click "Edit"
- Update fields and resubmit
- Website will update within 15 minutes

### Q: What if I want to delete an article?
**A**: Contact the tech team with the article title

### Q: Image upload failed
**A**:
- Check file size (must be under 5MB)
- Ensure format is JPG or PNG
- Try a different image
- Contact tech support if issue persists

## SEO Tips (For Better Discoverability)

### Headlines
- Include keywords (e.g., "HIV," "Mental Health," "Tanzania")
- Be specific ("New Training" vs. "Important Announcement")

### Excerpt
- Use 2-3 of your target keywords naturally

### Article Content
- First sentence should summarize the article
- Use subheadings (h2, h3) to break up text
- Include relevant statistics or quotes
- Link to related resources or previous articles

### Images
- File name should describe content (e.g., "hepatitis-screening-dar.jpg" not "image-2024-04-01.jpg")
- Alt text added automatically; it's the article headline
