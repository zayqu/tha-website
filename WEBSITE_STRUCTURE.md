## Content Hierarchy

### Most Important (Featured on Homepage)
1. **Programs/Campaigns** - Your main work
2. **Organization Story** - Why THA exists
3. **Impact** - What you've achieved
4. **Get Involved** - How people can help

### Important (Accessible from Navigation)
5. **About** - Detailed organization info
6. **Thematic Areas** - Focus on Hepatitis, HIV, Mental Health
7. **Projects** - Specific initiatives
8. **News** - Updates and announcements

### Supportive (In Footer/Links)
9. **Contact** - How to reach you
10. **Partners** - Your collaborators
11. **Team** - Your people
12. **Testimonials** - Social proof

## Color Usage Throughout

### Primary Blue (#024d85)
- Main headings
- Buttons (hover state)
- Navigation active state
- Featured callouts

### Secondary Green (#26b805)
- Call-to-action buttons
- Accent highlights
- Success indicators
- "Learn More" buttons

### Accent Orange (#ff9c1a)
- Special alerts
- Important notices
- Donation buttons
- Urgent CTAs

### Neutrals (Grays/Black)
- Body text
- Secondary headings
- Dividers
- Background sections
