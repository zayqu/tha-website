# THA News Posting Guide

## How to Post News in 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Go to News Form
Visit: **tha.or.tz/post-news**

### Step 2: Fill the Form
- **Title**: Write a clear headline
- **Category**: Select (Announcement/Event/Press Release/Success Story)
- **Content**: Write your article
- **Image**: Upload photo (automatically optimized)
- **Date**: Choose publish date

### Step 3: Submit
Click "Publish" - Your article goes live in 15 minutes!

## Tips
✓ Use clear, descriptive titles
✓ Upload high-quality images
✓ Proofread before publishing
✓ Choose the right category

## Image Guidelines
- Minimum size: 1200x800px
- Formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
- Maximum file size: 5MB
